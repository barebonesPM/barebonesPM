# BareBones PM

**Free property management software and rent ledger for DIY landlords.** [barebonespm.com](https://www.barebonespm.com)

BareBones PM is for people who manage their own rentals and want clean books without paying for a full property management suite. Track rent and expenses per property and unit, keep the receipt with every line, and have your Schedule E numbers ready at tax time.

**Built API-first.** The core of the app (properties, units, tenants, leases and the ledger) is available through a documented REST API ([docs](https://www.barebonespm.com/docs)), so you can post rent, bills and expenses from [n8n](https://www.barebonespm.com/integrations/n8n), [Zapier](https://www.barebonespm.com/integrations/zapier), [Make](https://www.barebonespm.com/integrations/make), Google Sheets or your own code.

**Works with AI assistants.** The [BareBones PM skill](https://www.barebonespm.com/skill) teaches Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Cursor how your ledger works, so an assistant can log expenses and answer questions about your rentals.

Guides for landlords: [rent ledger](https://www.barebonespm.com/resources/rent-ledger), [Schedule E explained](https://www.barebonespm.com/resources/schedule-e-explained), [deductible expenses](https://www.barebonespm.com/resources/deductible-expenses), [landlord glossary](https://www.barebonespm.com/glossary), [blog](https://www.barebonespm.com/blog).
